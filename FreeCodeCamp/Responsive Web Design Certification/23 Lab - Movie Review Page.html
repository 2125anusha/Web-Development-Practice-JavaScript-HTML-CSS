Animated Movie Review: Moana (2016)

Disney’s 2016 animated masterpiece Moana is a visually stunning and culturally rich adventure that breathes fresh life into the classic Disney formula. Driven by an unforgettable, soaring soundtrack co-written by Lin-Manuel Miranda, the film brilliantly captures the spirit of Polynesian wayfinding and myth. Auliʻi Cravalho delivers a deeply heartfelt vocal performance as Moana, beautifully complemented by Dwayne Johnson's infectious energy as the charismatic demigod Maui. Instead of a traditional romance, the narrative powerfully focuses on self-discovery, leadership, and a profound respect for ancestral legacy. Ultimately, its groundbreaking water animation and empowering heroine make it an enduring, universally loved classic for all generations.

Movie Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐☆☆ (8.1/10)
